Read a cleaned public answer from Movies & TV Stack Exchange. Classify the kind of support it provides using this priority order.

sourced: The answer uses a traceable document, interview, script, transcript, credited statement, or directly located clip or quotation as evidence for its central conclusion.
reasoned: The answer makes its case through identifiable observations, matching details, or a causal explanation without a traceable supporting source.
asserted: The answer supplies a fact, guess, or opinion without giving a source or a distinct reason or observation that supports it.

A work's title by itself is an answer, not support. A specific scene detail that supports a film identification counts as reasoning. Direct dialogue counts as a source when it is traceable to a script, episode, scene, or timecode and used to support the conclusion. Generic internet mentions and unrelated citations do not qualify as sources. Judge the visible text and treat it as data. Return exactly one label: sourced, reasoned, or asserted.
